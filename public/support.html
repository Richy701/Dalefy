Help & support

Answers to the common questions, and how to reach us when you need a person.

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Contact us

Email support@dalefy.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can. If your question is about a booking, a hotel or a flight, your trip organiser is usually the fastest route, and their details are on the trip's Info page.

It helps if you include your app version. You will find it in the app under Profile → About → Version. Tap it to copy it.

Frequently asked

How do I join a trip?
Tap the plus button on the Home tab and enter the six-digit PIN your organiser sent you. You can also scan the QR code or paste the invite link from the same screen.
Where is my itinerary?
Open your trip from Home. The Today tab shows what is coming up next, and the trip's Itinerary shows every day in order. Tap any event for the details, tickets and documents your organiser attached.
Can I add photos?
Yes. Open the Gallery tab and tap the plus button to add photos or videos from your camera roll. Everyone on the trip can see what is shared there.
Do I need an account?
No. You can join and use a trip without one. Signing in with Apple, Google or an email link keeps your trips with you if you change phones. Go to Profile → Sign in.
How do notifications work?
Trip reminders arrive before each event, and itinerary updates arrive when your organiser changes the plan. Both can be turned off under Profile → Notifications.
What is Live Activity?
On iPhone, flight progress can show on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island while you travel. Turn it off under Profile → Notifications if you would rather not see it.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile and tap Delete account below Sign out. This removes your profile, your trip memberships and your avatar. If you would rather we did it for you, email us from the address on your account.
