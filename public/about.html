Dalefy

Trips, planned and followed in one place

Dalefy is a trip companion app. Travel organisers build itineraries in a shared workspace, and travellers follow their trip live on their phone.

For travel organisers

Dalefy is the workspace where a travel team plans a trip end to end.

  • Build day-by-day itineraries with flights, stays, transfers and events.
  • Import bookings and confirmations, with flight details checked automatically.
  • Manage travellers, documents and compliance for each trip.
  • Publish the finished itinerary to travellers with your own branding.

For travellers

Travellers use the Dalefy mobile app to follow the trip their organiser planned.

  • See the full itinerary, updated live if plans change.
  • Get flight status and reminders for what's next.
  • Keep trip documents, maps and the shared photo gallery in one place.

Signing in

Dalefy is used by travel organisations and their clients. Access to the organiser workspace is by invitation from your team admin. Travellers join a trip from the link or code their organiser sends them.

Sign in with Google or with your email address. We only use your Google account to confirm who you are. See the privacy policy for what we store.
