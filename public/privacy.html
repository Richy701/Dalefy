Dalefy

Privacy policy

What Dalefy collects, why, and how to get it removed.

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Who we are

Dalefy is a trip companion app. Travel organisers publish itineraries to it, and travellers use it to follow their trip. When a trip is branded "Powered by" an organiser, that organiser planned the trip, and Dalefy provides the app.

What we collect

If you sign in: your name, email address and profile photo, as provided by Apple, Google or the email link you used. Signing in is optional.

Trip data: the trips you join, the traveller you link yourself to on a trip, and the photos and videos you add to a trip gallery.

Device data: a random identifier for this installation so your trips stay with the device, and a push notification token if you allow notifications. On iPhone, a Live Activity token lets flight progress show on your lock screen.

Location: the app does not track where you are. Event addresses set by your organiser are looked up on a map so the app can show them to you.

How we use it

  • To show you your trips, and keep them in sync across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To send trip reminders and itinerary updates you have opted into.
  • To share your name and avatar with the other people on the same trip.
  • To answer support requests you send us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Your trip organiser can see who has joined their trip and which traveller you linked to.
  • Google Firebase stores accounts, trip data and uploaded media, and delivers push notifications.
  • Mapbox provides maps and looks up the event addresses on a trip.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them, and Apple handles Live Activities.
  • Expo routes push notifications and app updates to your device.

All of this data travels over encrypted connections.

How long we keep it

Trip data stays for as long as the trip exists with the organiser. Your account and profile stay until you delete them. Photos you add to a gallery stay with that trip until you or the organiser remove them.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account. That removes your profile, your trip memberships and your avatar, and signs you out. Photos you added to a trip gallery can be removed from the gallery itself.

You can also email support@dalefy.com from the address on your account and we will do it for you.

Children

Dalefy is not aimed at children under 16. Younger travellers on a family or group trip are added by their organiser, who is responsible for having the right consent.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and let you know in the app. Questions go to support@dalefy.com.
